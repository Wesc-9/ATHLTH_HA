# ATHLTH Home Assistant v0.7.0 — Verified completed-set progression

This HACS update expands the ATHLTH iPad training dashboard with a
**real recorded strength progression** card built only from completed
working sets in **finished ATHLTH strength workouts**. Home Assistant's
left navigation sidebar remains available in the optional iPad kiosk view.

## New

- New `custom:athlth-strength-progress-card` in the main ATHLTH
  Training Intelligence page and the iPad kiosk **Detaljer** view.
- Switch between exercises and *exact repetition counts* with accessible
  buttons. View the sequence of best weights per finished workout and
  the best recorded value from the shared sample.
- The chart does not mix a 5-rep squat with a 10-rep squat.
- Live, planned, warm-up and incomplete sets are never counted.
- The iPhone exporter uses actual effort segments in mixed/dropsets:
  the system does not invent a higher-weight combined set.
- The ATHLTH app's separate `Styrkeprogresjon · fullførte sett`
  sharing permission defaults to OFF. Completed-workout sharing
  must also be enabled.
- Up to **60** completed per-workout best-effort points from the last
  90 days, with signed `strength_progress_synced` events, feature
  negotiation and explicit signed revocation.
- Per-paired-device persistence and sensor status are isolated.
- The Home Assistant sensor `sensor.athlth_strength_progress`
  contains only permitted fields. No route, actual workout ID,
  account secret or raw HealthKit samples enter the progression sensor.
- **Bug fix:** the normal signed webhook response now advertises
  `workout_history_v1` consistently; v0.6.0 could advertise it
  on duplicate deliveries but not normal deliveries.

## Important correctness limitations

- Strength weights and repetitions are logged in ATHLTH by the athlete.
  `completed_strength_set` attests that the *set was marked completed in
  a finished workout*, not that a calibrated sensor verified the weight.
- The best value is the highest recorded **among exported records**.
  Do not interpret it as an all-time PR if the app has earlier or
  unshared history.
- Only 60 points and 90 days are shared. A frequent lifter may
  have partial coverage, clearly disclosed by the UI.
- This release does **not** claim to support verified fastest 4×4 interval
  records or treadmill-speed achievement. The available generic
  completed-run summaries do not prove four actual completed work
  intervals at the stated achieved speed.
- The full [performance progression design contract](https://github.com/Wesc-9/ATHLTH_HA/blob/main/docs/PERFORMANCE_PROGRESSION_0_6_1.md)
  details necessary measured/manual/estimated provenance and
  4×4-specific completion data before such achievements can be shipped.
- As before, HA Recorder may preserve old health-related sensor states
  even after the integration clears current sharing state; users must
  manage Recorder retention separately.
- The **ATHLTH iPhone app must also include the matching exporter**.
  Updating HACS alone cannot populate strength progression.
  No TestFlight upload is initiated by the HACS release.

## Upgrade

1. In HACS, update **ATHLTH to v0.7.0**, then restart Home Assistant.
2. Keep your ATHLTH iPad kiosk or use the existing separate dashboard:
   [iPad kiosk example](https://github.com/Wesc-9/ATHLTH_HA/blob/main/examples/ipad-kiosk-dashboard.yaml).
3. The HA left sidebar remains available. The internal `kiosk: true`
   card setting **never** hides the Home Assistant shell.
4. After the corresponding iPhone app build is available and installed,
   opt into **Styrkeprogresjon · fullførte sett** in ATHLTH's Home
   Assistant sharing settings if you want these workout results visible.
5. See Styrkeprogresjon in the dashboard or its independent detail view.
6. Disable sharing to clear current ATHLTH progression. Audit Recorder
   separately if historical state snapshots also require purging.
